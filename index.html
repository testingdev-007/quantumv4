ACT FOUR · THE LEDGER, THE OTHER QUANTUM TRICK

The account number is a lock, not a label.

🕶️
VALEFlagged is not the same as usable. That account's real reference is masked behind a number — N = 91. Crack that, and you have a routing code the bank can act on tonight.

Not another search. A completely different quantum trick, for a completely different job: finding a hidden repeating pattern instead of finding one item in a list.

The idea: pick a number and keep multiplying it by itself, always keeping only the remainder after dividing by 91. Do that for long enough and the remainders start repeating — and the length of that repeat is enough, on its own, to split 91 back into the two numbers multiplied to make it. No guessing either number directly, however big it actually is.
Keep pressing until the remainders loop back round to 1.
3¹ mod 91 = ?
There — 3⁶ mod 91 = 1. The remainders take exactly 6 steps to loop back round. That length is the period.
Split that period in half (3) and compare 3³−1 and 3³+1 against 91, and the two hidden numbers just fall out.
Same wave-interference trick as Mark and Boost — spotting a pattern instead of tipping odds — which is exactly why it needs a quantum computer to do quickly. By hand, you multiplied this out step by step; a real bank reference number is hundreds of digits longer, and "step by step" stops being an option long before you get there.
91=7×13
